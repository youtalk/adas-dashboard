# Recordings

One directory per run, one JSONL file per endpoint, one message per line, the first line a `hello`. Play them with `python3 -m tools.replay stack=<dir>/stack.jsonl:8090 world=<dir>/world.jsonl:8091`, and add `supervisor=<dir>/supervisor.jsonl:8092` if the run has that file. To see the end of a run, do not add `--loop`. The files have different lengths, so a loop drifts them apart.

| Directory | Source | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `ces2027-kill-route/` | generated by `tools/synth.py` | 55 s total. Cruise at 12 m/s on a curving two-lane road. Lane departure warning (`warn`, target `lane_left`) from 12 to 14 s. Forward collision warning (`critical`, target `lead`) from 23.4 to 27.9 s. VisionPilot killed at 40 s, which ends `stack.jsonl`. The Safety Island takes authority at 40.5 s and stops the car by 44.5 s. Four objects: a lead car, a car with the `suv` height ratio, a `barrier` with no mesh, and a truck. The truck appears at 8 s and disappears at 30 s. |
| `ces2027-bench-board1/` | recorded by `tools/record.py` on the bench on 2026-10-27 | The kill route, live. VisionPilot runs on board 1 (R-Car X5H), the CARLA and Safety Island adapters on the CARLA host. 150 s. Until 6 s the Safety Island still holds the stop of the previous run, so the page starts in Stopped. The car starts at 7 s and cruises at 12 m/s on a multi-lane highway. VisionPilot sends no `lanes`. Its only alert is `LLDW` (`info`, target `trajectory`), from 11 to 20 s and from 21 to 22 s. Cars and trucks only, at most two in a frame. VisionPilot killed at 61 s, which ends `stack.jsonl`. The Safety Island takes authority at 67 s and stops the car at 72 s. `world.jsonl` ends at 108 s. |

The times count from the first message after the `hello` of each file, because `replay.py` starts each file there. On the bench, VisionPilot sent its first frame about 6 s after the recorder connected, and the other two endpoints sent theirs at once. The files have no common clock, so a replay cannot line them up. A replay of the bench run therefore shows Stack lost for about 6 s before the takeover. On the bench the first Safety Island command came 0.64 s after the kill.

The generated run stays. Only it plays through all six states of design section 6.3, and the replay smoke test (`src/replay.test.ts`) and CI depend on it. Recordings are CC0.
